Scope of Engagement

Five Consultation Pillars

Each pillar below is a distinct clause of service — engaged individually or combined into a full advisory retainer, depending on where your organization needs the most support.

§1

Clinical & Therapeutic Science

Translating peer-reviewed cannabis pharmacology and therapeutics research into protocols your clinical or product team can actually operate on — dosage frameworks, indication mapping, and evidence review that holds up to scrutiny.

Clinical Protocols Evidence Review Dosage Frameworks
§2

Regulatory & Compliance Mapping

Cross-referencing state and federal requirements so your operation stays audit-ready across every jurisdiction you touch. This includes legislative drafting support and documentation built to survive a state inspection.

Multi-State Compliance Audit Readiness Legislative Drafting
§3

Corporate Strategy & Board Advisory

Serving as a scientific liaison or strategic voice at the board level — structuring brand-licensing partnerships, evaluating M&A targets, and advising leadership on where the regulatory landscape is headed.

Board Advisory Brand Licensing Strategic Planning
§4

Supply Chain & Operations

Modeling lean, compliant supply chains from cultivation or manufacturing through distribution — upstream sourcing, downstream logistics, and the operational controls that keep both sides defensible.

Supply Chain Design Logistics Operational Controls
§5

AI & Workflow Automation

Embedding workflow automation into regulated operations without loosening the audit trail — SOP drafting, data tracking, and compliance-variance prediction, built for teams that answer to regulators.

SOP Automation Data Tracking Audit-Safe AI
